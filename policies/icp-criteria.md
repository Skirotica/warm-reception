# ICP Criteria

## In scope sectors
- Legaltech

## Company size
- Preferred employees: 50 to 350 inclusive
- A known headcount below 50 or above 350 is outside the preferred band. Note that on the case. Do not No-go and do not drop the company for headcount alone. The company stays in the queue and can still be a Go. Say how many people were found, and that the company is still included.

## Revenue
- Estimated revenue: $15M to $50M inclusive
- On live Exa cases, revenue is often blank on the company record even when Series A/B funding is clear. If sector is legaltech and VC/PE backing is yes, do **not** No-go solely for missing revenue, and do **not** No-go solely because headcount is outside 50 to 350. Score sector, backing, and event. Note headcount when it is outside the preferred band.
- If after all sources (company record, Exa event, Exa size enrichment) you still cannot tell whether the company is in or out of ICP, route **Table 3 Ambiguous**, not Table 2 No-go. No-go only when you **know** a disqualifier other than headcount (wrong sector, no VC/PE, bankruptcy, existing client, mega-round, etc.).

## Backing
- Must be VC or PE backed

## Talent acquisition headcount
- 0 to 2 inclusive

## Out of ICP
Any company failing sector, backing, or a hard stop is No-go (Table 2) unless routed earlier by a hard stop (existing client, bankruptcy, etc.). Headcount outside 50 to 350 is a note, not a No-go.

## Pre-qualified whitelist exception
If the company appears on `prequalified_whitelist.csv`, do **not** No-go for failing the size / revenue / backing / TA checks above. Treat ICP as already satisfied by partner vetting, then continue with event monitoring, POC, and draft rules. See `prequalified-whitelist.md`. Hard stops still apply.
