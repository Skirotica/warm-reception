# Black Gavel Outreach Sequence (Emails 1–4)

Synthetic voice reference for the generator. Tokens: {{first_name}}, {{company}}, {{CV1}}, {{CV2}}.

## Cadence
- Email 1 — trigger day (congratulate / open)
- Email 2 — ~3 days later (value / proof)
- Email 3 — ~7 days later (soft bump)
- Email 4 — ~14 days later (polite close)

All four emails are drafted together for human review. This file is not a live drip scheduler.
